PVL Ambassadors

PVL Ambassadors

PVL Ambassadors are logistics, transport and port professionals linked to Escola Europea – Intermodal Transport who carry its mission into their own institutions, regions and networks. They represent the Escola and its values, and help more educators and organisations bring innovation and quality, hands-on training into the port and logistics sector through Port Virtual Lab.

What they do

  • Represent Escola Europea and Port Virtual Lab in their institution, region or network.
  • Open doors: connect PVL with schools, universities, port communities and companies.
  • Share their experience in webinars, Training of Trainers editions, events and the CHIRON community.
  • Bring back ideas from the field so PVL keeps improving.

Who they are

Professionals with real experience in the sector, a close relationship with Escola Europea and a shared commitment to its values. The Escola appoints each PVL Ambassador.

How someone becomes an Ambassador

The formal appointment criteria are being written: what is asked of an Ambassador, for how long, and what the role gives back.

Interested, or want to propose someone? Write to international@escolaeuropea.eu.
