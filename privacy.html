Cookies

Cookie consent information

This website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and to help us understand how visitors interact with our content through Google Analytics. By default, no cookies that collect personal data are activated. We only use analytics cookies after you give your explicit consent. These cookies help us improve the performance and usability of our website, but they do not track you across other sites.

By clicking “Accept cookies” you agree to the use of analytics cookies on this site. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies.

What is stored before you accept

  • One entry in your own browser recording your cookie choice, so you are not asked again.
  • Your best score, if you play Container Stacker. It stays in your browser.

Neither leaves your device, and neither reaches Escola Europea.

Forms

What happens when
you fill a form

Demo and proposal requests

The name, email, institution and country you enter are used to answer your request and to prepare a proposal. Nothing else.

Training of Trainers

Your details register you for an edition and send you the access code. Free for teachers of universities and training institutes.

Certificate checks

A reference number is checked against the certificate register. No personal data is needed to run a check.

On this prototype, no form sends anything. Every form on the site is interface only: it validates what you type and then stops. Nothing is transmitted or stored on a server.

Your rights

Access, correction
and deletion

You can ask what data Escola Europea holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to international@escolaeuropea.eu and the team will answer.
