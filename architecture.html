Common base

What all three share

Identity and access, the content model, the design system, the notification layer and the audit trail. One base keeps the three surfaces consistent as the product grows.

Boundaries

How a user always knows where they are

Light = public

White surfaces, wide spacing, big messages. Marketing navigation with a demo call to action.

Navy = working

Dark header, dense layout, filters, statuses and progress. Task-first, no selling.

Amber = managing

The same navy shell with an amber accent: tables, records, permissions and operational health.

Leaving the ecosystem is always announced. When a company or tool lives on its own website, the interface states the destination, whether credentials are needed and how to come back — before the jump.
