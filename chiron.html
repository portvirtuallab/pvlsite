CHIRON Programme

The educators behind
every simulation

The CHIRON Programme is PVL’s train-the-trainer programme and the community of educators who teach with PVL.

Join the next Training of Trainers → Enter CHIRON ↗

Free for teachers of universities and training institutes.

Available

Part of the CHIRON Programme, run by Escola Europea – Intermodal Transport.

The problem

Finding educators who know
how ports really work

Port authorities and training institutions share one challenge: finding educators who know how port operations really work. CHIRON gives trainers and programme coordinators the method, the platform skills and the scenarios to run transport simulations with confidence, and a network of peers doing the same.

Who it is for

  • Teachers of universities and training institutes
  • Trainers
  • Programme coordinators
Three pillars

What the programme gives you

01Method

Methodology

Webinars, one-to-one support and an active network of educators teaching the same way.

02Platform

Platform mastery

Tutorials of PVL.ONE from the facilitator’s side, not the participant’s.

03Scenarios

Scenario design

Programmes and case studies tailored to each curriculum.

What is the access code?

Once you register, Escola Europea sends you a personal access code by email. You use it to enter the CHIRON space and, later, to open PVL.ONE from the facilitator’s side. It is tied to your institutional email, so it is not transferable.
