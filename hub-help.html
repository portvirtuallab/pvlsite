Frequent questions

I cannot open a module

Check that your cohort has it open — locked modules show the date. If it should be open, send us the code and your course name.

A company website asks for a login

External companies never need separate credentials for your cohort. If one asks, it is a configuration error — report it and use the alternative your trainer gives you.

Can I work from home?

Yes. Everything runs in a browser. Some live sessions are scheduled with your cohort, and those are in the calendar.

Is my progress stored?

Deliverables and team activity are visible to your trainer. This prototype does not display any personal record it cannot actually produce.

Found a broken link? Tell us where you found it. External destinations are checked automatically in CI, but a link can still expire between checks.
