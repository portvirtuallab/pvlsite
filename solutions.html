How they combine

Most institutions
climb the ladder

You do not have to start at the top. Each step tells you whether the next one is worth it.

  1. 01FlashOne session, no commitment. Your group feels what the job is like and you see the platform work.
  2. 02PVL Immersion CourseNine days inside your programme, facilitated, with your teachers trained alongside the students.
  3. 03Open LabThe platform is yours for a year. Your teachers run it, design cases and repeat with every cohort.
The process

From first call
to first session

Five steps. The first two cost you nothing but an hour.

01

Demo

A one-hour session with your programme on the table. We show the platform against your use case, not a generic tour.

02

Proposal

A written proposal for the format that fits: what is included, the dates we can hold and the investment.

03

Set-up

Accounts, the scenario built against your learning outcomes, and your teachers trained from the facilitator’s side.

04

Start

The first session runs with support behind you throughout.

05

Follow-up

Results, debrief material and what to change for the next cohort.

Investment

What the price
depends on

Every quote is built for one institution, which is why there is no list price. Four things move it.

  • Participants. How many people, and in how many teams.
  • Duration. A three-hour session and a nine-day course are different animals.
  • Modules. One sector of the chain, or an intermodal journey across several.
  • Facilitation. Whether Escola Europea runs it, or your own teachers do after training.

Tell us those four and you get a written proposal, with a validity date.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. A demo is an hour, and the Training of Trainers is free for teachers of universities and training institutes.

Do our teachers need training?

For Flash, no — Escola Europea facilitates. For the Immersion Course it is included. For Open Lab, your teachers run it, so the CHIRON Training of Trainers comes first.

Can we design our own cases?

With Open Lab, yes. The Immersion Course is tailored to your institution by Escola Europea.

What happens after the twelve months?

Renewal terms are to be confirmed. Ask in the proposal and it will be stated in writing.
