Nine modules

Activate one,
or build the journey

Each module replicates a specific sector of the port-logistics chain. Combine them for a fully intermodal training journey.

MAR↗Sea

Maritime

Sea freight, port operations, vessel scheduling, containerisation.

 AIR↗Air

Airfreight

Time-sensitive cargo, AWB, packaging, IATA documentation.

 RWY↗Rail

Railway

Intermodal rail, EU corridors, SDG railway operations.

 HAU↗Road

Haulier

Last-mile transport, CMR, dispatch, terminal coordination.

 INL↗Inland waterway

Inland navigation

River transport, barge operations, cargo handling, terminal coordination.

 CUS↗Trade

Customs

Customs clearance, documentation control, tariff management, border coordination.

 INS↗Risk

Insurance

Cargo insurance, risk assessment, claims handling, policy coordination.

 NEG↗Commercial

Negotiation

Deal negotiation, persuasive offers, case-study simulation, final evaluation.

 BMB↗Decision-making

BOMB dashboard

Business decisions under pressure: incidents, disruption, KPI analysis.
