Typography

Sora for display and headings — geometric, technical, a little more distinctive than the usual interface sans. Inter for body and UI, as in the brand guidelines. Both cover Spanish, Catalan, French and Italian accents.

Shape & depth

Radii 12 / 20 / 28. Shadows always blue-tinted, never black. Motion between 0.2s and 0.3s, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. Maximum content width 1216px.

Accessibility rules

  • Real buttons, links and labels — never a clickable div
  • Visible cyan focus ring, offset from the control
  • Text contrast 4.5:1, touch targets 44px and up
  • Skip link, landmarks and one H1 per page
Note on the amber. The original code defined a --lime variable holding the same amber and still used a leftover green in some effects. Here amber is the single active-state colour and the green only survives as a success badge.
