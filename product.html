The platform

A logistics lab, not
a course player

PVL.ONE is a comprehensive web-based platform offering technological and educational development tools: a logistics lab where students and professionals in international trade, logistics and transport simulate real operations and use the tools of the trade to develop and perfect their knowledge.

Constructivist approach

Learning is built,
not delivered

Constructivist learning is student-centred, built on discovery and inquiry. Instructors act as facilitators, not just content providers, which fosters deep understanding and real engagement.

01

Real-world experience

We learn through real-world experiences and interactions — every task in the platform mirrors an operational decision someone makes for a living.

02

Active participants

Students are active participants in their own learning. Nobody watches a simulation happen: each team has responsibilities the others depend on.

03

Language matters

Language plays a crucial role in cognitive development. Negotiating, explaining and documenting in English is part of the competence.

Digitalised document flow

Study cases become
real paperwork

Quotations, packing lists, commercial invoices and customs documents are generated and exchanged inside the simulation environment — through the Click & Cargo ERP, exactly as companies do it.

  • Quotations and commercial offers built from real cost structures
  • Packing lists, commercial invoices and transport documents
  • Customs documentation, tariff management and border coordination
  • Every document traceable to the team and the decision behind it

Supporting educators

Empowering educators,
enabling impact

Teachers are the cornerstone of every successful PVL experience. Content can match your curriculum and learning outcomes, and we work closely with educators before, during and after the course to ensure lasting value.

The Chiron programme

Before

Scenario design against your learning outcomes, plus platform tutorials from the facilitator’s side.

During

Facilitation support while the simulation runs, with the team reachable throughout the session.

After

Debrief material, results and access to an active network of educators running the same modules.

Where it is going

From project tool to
digital infrastructure

Port Virtual World extends the lab into operational digital twins of port-logistics systems.

Digital twins

Designed for training as working operational twins, mirroring real port community flows, decisions and operations.

Decision environments

Risk-free simulation, collective decision-making and interoperability across stakeholders and transport modes.

Scalable platform

Evolution from a project tool into a network generator for designing green corridors and efficient port communities.

Port Virtual World and Port Virtual Game are announced as coming soon; Hermes Lab and Trade Bank are in development. They are presented here as roadmap, never as available features.
