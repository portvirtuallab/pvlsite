How it runs

Six steps, and the last
one is where it lands

The shape of every session, whatever the module. The teacher facilitates; the teams decide.

01

Teams and roles

Each team joins the platform as an independent company, with a role brief and real responsibilities the other teams depend on.

02

The case

A shipment with a route, a cargo, a deadline and a cost structure, built from port community practice.

03

Quotation

Teams price the operation and negotiate. The offer that wins has to hold up commercially.

04

Documentation

The file is opened in Click & Cargo and the documents are issued, exchanged and checked, exactly as companies do it.

05

The incident

Something fails, as it does in a real port. A mismatch, a delay, a missing document. The teams have to resolve it.

06

Debrief

What each team decided, what it cost the others, and what they would do differently. This is where the experience becomes learning.

Digitalised document flow

Study cases become
real paperwork

Quotations, packing lists, commercial invoices and customs documents are generated and exchanged inside the simulation environment — through the Click & Cargo ERP, exactly as companies do it.

  • Quotations and commercial offers built from real cost structures
  • Packing lists, commercial invoices and transport documents
  • Customs documentation, tariff management and border coordination
  • Every document traceable to the team and the decision behind it

Practical information

What your institution
needs to run it

Short answer: a browser and an internet connection. The detail below is being confirmed with the product team.

Access

Web-based. Nothing to install, nothing to maintain on your side, and no licence per computer.

Technical requirements

A current browser and a normal classroom connection. The supported browser versions and any network requirements are to be confirmed.

Languages

Sessions run in EN · ES. The languages of the interface and the materials are to be confirmed.

Accounts

Open Lab includes 20 accounts. How accounts are issued for the other formats, and what happens at the end of a course, is to be confirmed.
