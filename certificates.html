Certificates

Check a certificate

Certificates issued after a PVL.ONE course carry a reference number. Enter it to confirm that it was issued by Escola Europea – Intermodal Transport, and for which course.

Verification

Public check
Printed at the bottom of the certificate, for example PVL-2026-0418-EN.
No personal data is stored by this check.
Valid certificate.
Course: Intermodal Operations 2026 · Issued 18 April 2026 · Escola Europea – Intermodal Transport.
Example of a successful result.

What the check tells you

  • That the reference exists and was issued by Escola Europea
  • Which course and edition it belongs to
  • The issue date and the language of the certificate

It does not publish grades, personal data or contact details of the holder.

Reference not found. Check the spelling, or write to international@escolaeuropea.eu with a copy of the document. Example of the failure state.
Prototype note. This checker is an interface only. The verification service, the reference format and which courses issue certificates are pending confirmation by the product owner.
